Our story

From Isabela
to every snack table.

3 Sher's Food Products is a family-owned business from San Fermin, Cauayan City, Isabela. Founded in 1989 by Sergio and Lorna Gonzales, the company grew from a family food business into a trusted name in fried corn snacks.

Today, Big Brothers Corniks continues that legacy with crunchy Filipino flavors made for pasalubong, gatherings and everyday merienda.
